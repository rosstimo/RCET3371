# Section 12 — Collaborative Development

## Outcomes

You should be able to:

- work safely in a shared repository;
- use branches, pull requests, review, and integration intentionally;
- diagnose and resolve merge conflicts;
- use issues/milestones to make work visible;
- preserve a trustworthy main/release line;
- provide evidence of your own contribution and review work.

## Practice
