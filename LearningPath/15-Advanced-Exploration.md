# Section 15 — Advanced Exploration

## Outcomes

You should be able to extend an existing system using one advanced software-system technique and explain the tradeoff it introduces.

The complete course provides supported paths in:

- automated testing and regression;
- asynchronous/concurrent event handling;
- networking/HTTP/JSON;
- packaging and continuous integration;
- deeper memory/compiler/disassembly analysis.

You are not expected to master every option. Complete the approved option specified by the course offering or choose one when the assignment permits choice.

## Learn

- [Advanced exploration](../Topics/advanced-exploration.md)

## Practice

Compare two implementation choices, identify their failure modes, and justify which fits a stated engineering constraint.

## Programming Assignment

Complete the advanced-extension component of the [Integration/Verification Portfolio](../ProgrammingAssignments/IntegrationVerificationPortfolio/README.md).

## Assessment

Section 15 assessment emphasizes reasoning, evidence, and transfer rather than memorizing every advanced option.
