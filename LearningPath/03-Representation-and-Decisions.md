# Section 3 — Representation and Decisions

## Outcomes

You should be able to:

- reason about value, representation, type, range, signedness, and overflow;
- use arithmetic, comparison, logical, bitwise, shift, mask, and conversion operations;
- extract and insert packed fields;
- reconstruct multi-byte values;
- use engineering notation, metric prefixes, and significant figures appropriately;
- keep full internal precision while formatting a rounded display;
- compare the same representation problem across C#, Python, embedded C, and PIC assembly.
